=====================================

Tool: WHOIS Lookup

Objective:
To gather publicly available domain registration details,
including domain age, registrar, expiry date, and DNS
configuration.

- Domain Creation Date: 06 November 2019
- Last Updated: 12 November 2025
- Expiry Date: 06 November 2027
- Registrar: GoDaddy.com, LLC
- Domain Status: Client Transfer, Update, Renew and Delete Prohibited

Observation:
The WHOIS lookup revealed the domain's registration
timeline, registrar, DNS name servers, and domain status.

a domain privacy service.

Conclusion:
This exercise provided practical experience in domain
reconnaissance and understanding how WHOIS information
can reveal publicly available domain registration and
DNS-related details.
